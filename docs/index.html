Bio

That Boy Hi Hat is an alternative rap artist from Frederick, Maryland, managed by Cumulative Web Inc.

His breakout single "Zooted Zone" was produced by Kokurcho, mixed and mastered by Hybrid at his Hagerstown, Maryland studio, and recorded in Hagerstown in February 2023.

His 2026 single "Diabolique" was recorded at Cue Recording Studio in Arlington, Virginia, and is the focus of a Spotify Discovery Mode campaign scheduled for October 1–31, 2026 (approved September 11, 2026).
